Download

Beyond the website, this edition is published in three forms. The data, free; the e-book and the programmatic interface, for a modest price.

---The data.--- The complete text is available as a [bulk download](/download/eb1911-corpus.tar.gz) or on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/britannica11/eb1911), under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), from its [source](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/1911_Encyclopædia_Britannica). Each of the 36,691 articles is one record. Its text is in Markdown, with metadata, sections, and cross-references. The download includes three indexes: the *cross-reference graph*, joining every article to those it names; the *topic index*, classifying more than 90% of the articles by subject; and the *contributor graph*, resolving each author's initials to names, credentials, and a biographical article, where available. The topic index is faithfully reproduced from the original; the other two are unique to this edition. The plain text can of course be had anywhere.

---The book.--- The entire encyclopedia is available as [a single e-book](https://payhip.com/b/ifyaR): all 36,691 articles and the more than 10,000 illustrations in one DRM-free EPUB, with maximum fidelity to the original. It is fully cross-linked, can be browsed by topic and by volume, and includes instant search of the corpus, full-text and by title, with no connection required. $39.99; corrected editions free to purchasers. Volume 1 is [free](/download/eb1911-vol01.epub). A Kindle edition will be ready shortly.

---The interface.--- A programmatic interface, which will include full-text search, article retrieval, and traversal of the topics, contributors, and cross-references, is in preparation.

---The maps.--- The edition's full-color fold-out maps — the celebrated Perthes maps among them — are collected in a separate [maps bundle](/download/eb1911-maps.tar.gz), free of charge: the plates as printed in 1911 and, where we have them, the Stieler Hand-Atlas originals they were engraved from, at full resolution. The same maps can be browsed [here](/ancillary.html).
